Checking sign-in…

Four short guides, one per role — each shows only the steps that role actually does. You're seeing the one(s) that match your own sign-in below; an Admin or Super Admin sees all four, since supporting everyone else's workflow is part of the job.

Admin — full workflow

Everything below, start to finish, plus the accounts and records behind it all.

1

Add the Part, if it's new

Part Number is the only required field. Link the Customer and any Suppliers who make it, and attach the drawing if you have one.

Open Parts
2

Log the Customer PO

Enter the header (customer, dates, terms) and every line item, then attach the customer's original PDF for reference. The line total and PO total are calculated for you as you type.

Open Customer POs
3

A Dessimate PO goes out to the supplier

A Team Member issues the Dessimate PO — PO Number and Shipment Number are both assigned automatically the moment it's saved, and can't be typed or changed afterward.

Open Dessimate POs
4

PDIR and Supplier Invoice come in

The PDIR is filled under that Shipment Number, and the Supplier Invoice is recorded once it arrives — linked to the Dessimate PO it belongs to.

Open PDIR Portal
5

Track pre-production sign-off, if this Part needs it

The 9-item APQP checklist (Design Record, Control Plan, PSW, and so on) — files and a comment log, per Part.

Open APQP
6

Issue the Dessimate Invoice

Reference the Customer PO — Invoice Number is assigned automatically on save. Mark it Paid once payment is received; deleting one is a soft delete, so the numbering sequence never has a gap.

Open Dessimate Invoices
7

Manage the people and organizations behind all of this

Users — accounts, access levels, and each Approver's stamp image. Organizations — every Supplier and Customer's contact info and documents. Both are Super Admin only; Parts is open to every signed-in Dessimate team member already.

Open Users

Dessimate Team Member — Dessimate PO through Supplier Invoice

Starts once a Customer PO already exists in the system, and stops before the Dessimate Invoice — that part's an Admin step.

1

Issue the Dessimate PO

Pick the Supplier and the Part(s) being ordered. PO Number and Shipment Number are assigned automatically when you save — if your account has an Approver's stamp set, it's embedded on the generated PDF.

Open Dessimate POs
2

Fill the PDIR

Under the same Shipment Number the Dessimate PO just created.

Open PDIR Portal
3

Record the Supplier Invoice

Once the supplier's invoice arrives, log it here and link it to the Dessimate PO it belongs to.

Open Supplier Invoices
4

Keep the Parts master current

Add new part numbers as they come up, attach drawings, and keep each part's Customer/Supplier links accurate — read and write here is open to every signed-in Dessimate team member.

Open Parts
5

Track pre-production sign-off

Upload documents and leave comments against the 9-item APQP checklist for any Part that needs it.

Open APQP

Supplier — your PO through your invoice

Everything from your side of one shipment, in order.

Supplier sign-in isn't turned on yet — this is what you'll see once it is.
1

You receive a Dessimate PO

It carries a PO Number and a Shipment Number — this is the order to fulfill.

2

Ship against it

The Shipment Number is how everything downstream — the PDIR, your invoice — ties back to this specific order.

3

Your input feeds the PDIR

The quality inspection report for that shipment, filed under its Shipment Number.

4

Send your invoice

Once it's sent, Dessimate records it against this same Dessimate PO and shipment.

Customer — your PO through your invoice

What happens to your order after you send it, and what you'll be able to see.

Customer sign-in isn't turned on yet — this is what you'll see once it is.
1

Your PO is logged

Once Dessimate receives it, it's entered against your organization with every line item.

2

Dessimate places the order with a manufacturing supplier

On your behalf, and manages the shipment through to delivery.

3

You can review the PDIR

The quality inspection report for your shipment — everything on it except the Supplier Invoice, which stays internal to Dessimate.

4

Dessimate invoices you

Referencing your original PO number.
